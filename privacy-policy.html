Information We Collect

When you fill out one of our forms, we collect only what you provide:

Your form responses are processed through Meta's lead ads platform (Facebook/Instagram), which is governed by Meta's own privacy practices. Beyond that, we share your information only when required by law.

Our program serves children ages 5–13, but we collect contact information from parents and guardians only — never directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us information directly, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond to your request and for reasonable business records afterward. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, call (689) 276-0250 or email windermerefl@ucmas-usa.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this page with its effective date.
