Build your child's future with UCMAS

Universal Concept of Mental Arithmetic System (UCMAS) Windermere teaches children ages 5–13 to calculate mentally using the abacus, building concentration, memory, visualization, and confidence. Classes run in Windermere, Florida.

Our Program

Children start by learning to calculate on a physical abacus. With regular practice, they begin to picture the beads in their minds and solve problems mentally — without a calculator and without counting on their fingers. The program is structured by level, and students progress as they master each stage.

UCMAS is a global abacus and mental math program. Our Windermere center serves families in Windermere and the surrounding Orlando area.

What children build

  • Focus and concentration — following bead movements and holding numbers in mind
  • Memory and visualization — picturing the abacus to solve problems mentally
  • Calculation speed — answering arithmetic faster with practice
  • Confidence — mastering levels step by step, in class and on stage

Results vary by student; we do not guarantee specific academic outcomes.
